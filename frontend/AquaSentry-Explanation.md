# AquaSentry

## What it is

AquaSentry is a water-safety monitoring system for homes and small communities. It uses a sensor station or minibot to check water quality, identify unusual contaminant levels, and present clear next steps when a reading may be unsafe.

## What the dashboard shows

- The latest detected contaminant and its measured value.
- The relevant safe limit and confidence in the reading.
- A possible source of the issue and nearby-station confirmation.
- Immediate household safety guidance.
- Treatment options and a report summary for local authorities.
